What are the possibilities of AI? My thoughts about the future.

For about three months I have used spool-hub.ai. For a few dozen euros a month, I feel I have access to some of the world's best experts. Four thoughts:

⚖️ AI will eliminate jobs, but not evenly. I predict fewer lawyers and possibly more software developers. If a contract review costs a hundred times less, we will not review a hundred times more contracts. But when software becomes ten times cheaper, we will build vastly more of it.

⚡ The revolution in physical power took about 150 years. Machine thinking arrived in four: almost 40 times faster, and it is already on our phones. I believe this is the fastest productivity revolution in history.

📈 AI has increased my own cognitive output tenfold: twice as many things, each five times more deeply. This is not a zero-sum game. Small, ambitious businesses gain the most, and if we use AI merely to preserve existing organisations and jobs, we have misunderstood the revolution.

🤝 AI is becoming a friend. It listens, it does not interrupt, and it encourages. If someone feels heard, why should it matter whether the listener is human?

🧭 Afraid AI will take your job? Do one thing: use it every day, with curiosity and a little greed, and learn to recognise its mistakes.

💡 What if the greatest impact of AI is not how much less people will need to do, but how much more they will be able to achieve?
