## What it is

The Spool interface is now fully localized and supports 19 different languages. Whether you are using English, Spanish, Finnish, or Hebrew, the platform natively accommodates your preferred language right from the start, with robust right-to-left support where applicable.

## Why use it

A truly international tool should speak your language. This update ensures that team members across the globe can collaborate in the language they are most comfortable with, making navigation, issue tracking, and messaging more accessible and inclusive for everyone.

## How to use it

To change your interface language, click on your avatar in the top-right corner to open your User Settings, and navigate to the **Language** section. Select your preferred language from the searchable list and press Save. Your choice is remembered and applied across all your devices the next time you sign in.

## The old way

Previously, the platform was only available in English. Teams working in different locales had to adapt to English interfaces, potentially slowing down work and making onboarding harder for non-native speakers.

## The new way

Now, you can immediately switch your workspace to one of 19 languages. The entire interface re-renders instantly, allowing you to focus on your tasks without any language barriers getting in the way.
