## What it is

The Archive feature allows you to freeze inactive workspaces, channels, or topics. When archived, they are removed from the active sidebar but remain fully searchable and accessible for future reference.

## Why use it

Digital clutter can overwhelm teams, making it harder to focus on current priorities. Archiving lets you clear away completed projects or outdated discussions without losing valuable historical context or decisions.

## How to use it

Click the settings icon next to a workspace, channel, or topic, and select "Archive". It will instantly disappear from the main navigation for all members. To view it again, navigate to the "Archived" section in your account settings.

## The old way

Users typically had to choose between permanently deleting old data to clean up the interface, risking the loss of important information, or leaving everything active, which led to a bloated and confusing navigation pane.

## The new way

Archiving offers the best of both worlds. It keeps your daily environment clean and focused on active work, while ensuring that past discussions and shared files are securely stored and easily searchable whenever you need them.
