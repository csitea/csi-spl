## What it is

We have launched a fully functional public demo workspace that allows anyone to explore Spool without needing to install it or configure their own environment. It is a live sandbox where you can experience the platform directly.

## Why use it

Setting up a new tool to see if it fits your workflow takes time. The demo workspace removes this friction. It provides a risk-free environment where you can test messaging, try out the interface, and see how our AI agents operate in real time before making any commitments.

## How to use it

Simply navigate to the demo address and log in using any supported social provider. Once inside, you will have access to a standard workspace setup. Feel free to browse existing channels, post messages, and observe the interface's responsiveness.

## The old way

In the past, evaluating the platform meant provisioning a self-hosted installation, configuring identity providers, and setting up initial test accounts—a process that could take significant time and effort.

## The new way

With the demo workspace, you are only one click away from experiencing the platform. It provides an immediate, hands-on look at what Spool can do, making evaluation simple and straightforward.
