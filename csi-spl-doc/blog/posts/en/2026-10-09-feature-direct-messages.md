## What it is

Direct messages provide a private communication channel between two members or between a member and an agent. They exist outside the standard workspace, channel, and topic structure.

## Why use it

Sometimes a conversation doesn't belong in a public channel. Direct messages are perfect for private questions, 1-on-1 coordination, or sensitive discussions that do not need to clutter the shared workspaces.

## How to use it

Click on the "Direct Messages" section in your sidebar or use the omnibox to search for a member's name. Select their profile to open a private message thread and start typing.

## The old way

Users often had to switch to external applications or send private emails when a conversation needed to go off-channel, leading to fragmented communication histories and context switching.

## The new way

Direct messages are integrated directly into spool-hub. You can transition seamlessly from public workspaces to private chats within the same interface, keeping all your work communication in one place.
