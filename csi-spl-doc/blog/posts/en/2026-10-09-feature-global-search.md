## What it is

Global Search is a powerful, system-wide search engine built into Spool. It scans across all your workspaces, channels, topics, and direct messages to find exactly what you are looking for in milliseconds.

## Why use it

As your organization grows, so does the amount of information stored in your communication platform. Global Search prevents you from losing important data and saves you from manually clicking through multiple channels to find a specific file or conversation.

## How to use it

Press the designated keyboard shortcut or click the search bar at the top of the interface to open Global Search. Type in your query, and filter the results by workspace, sender, date, or file type to narrow down the exact information you need.

## The old way

Users often had to remember exactly which channel or direct message thread a conversation happened in, scrolling endlessly through chat history to find a past decision or document.

## The new way

With Global Search, everything is indexed and instantly retrievable. You can query the entire platform from anywhere, making your team's collective knowledge accessible at a moment's notice.
