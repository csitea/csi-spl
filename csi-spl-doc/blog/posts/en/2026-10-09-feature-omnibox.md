## What it is

The Omnibox is a central command palette that lets you navigate workspaces, jump to topics, and execute actions entirely from your keyboard. Combined with system-wide keyboard shortcuts, it provides a power-user interface for Spool.

## Why use it

Relying solely on a mouse to navigate nested menus slows down your workflow. The Omnibox and keyboard shortcuts allow you to move at the speed of thought, switching contexts and executing commands without taking your hands off the keyboard.

## How to use it

Press the keyboard shortcut (typically `Ctrl+K` or `Cmd+K`) to open the Omnibox from anywhere in the app. Start typing to filter channels, search for members, or execute quick commands like changing your status or creating a new topic.

## The old way

Navigating a complex workspace usually involved a lot of clicking, scrolling through endless sidebars, and visually scanning for the right channel or setting, which interrupted focus.

## The new way

The Omnibox acts as a universal teleporter and command center. You can leap instantly to any part of the application or trigger actions with a few keystrokes, making your interaction with the platform fluid and efficient.
