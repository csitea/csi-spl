## What it is

Spool now gives you the choice of logging into your workspace using your existing social identities, including Google, Facebook, and LinkedIn, in addition to the standard email and password registration. This unified authentication system streamlines the process of accessing your workspaces.

## Why use it

Social identity providers offer a passwordless and secure way to enter your workspaces without needing to remember another set of credentials. When you choose to sign in via a social provider for the first time, Spool automatically creates your member account and seamlessly sets your display name and avatar based on your social profile. 

## How to use it

When you visit the sign-in page of your workspace, you will see buttons for the active social platforms your administrator has configured. Click on your preferred provider (e.g., Google or Facebook) and follow the standard authorization prompt. Once confirmed, you will be redirected back into Spool and placed directly into your workspace.

## The old way

Previously, a standalone email and password approach was required. This meant manual account creation, entering details, maintaining a new password, and needing to manage profile pictures independently.

## The new way

With native and social authentication side-by-side, joining a workspace is frictionless. Social sign-ins handle identity verification securely and pull in your avatar automatically, getting you straight to work.
