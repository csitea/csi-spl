## What it is

Workspaces, channels, and topics form the core organizational structure in Spool. Workspaces contain channels for broad subjects, and channels contain topics for specific conversations, keeping all discussions neatly categorized.

## Why use it

Without a clear structure, team communication quickly becomes chaotic. This three-tiered approach ensures that discussions stay on topic, information is easy to find, and notifications only reach those who need them.

## How to use it

To get started, create a workspace for your team. Inside the workspace, create channels for projects or departments. Within a channel, start a topic for any specific discussion. You can browse this structure using the sidebar.

## The old way

In traditional chat applications, everything was often thrown into a few large channels, leading to overwhelming noise and important decisions getting lost in the scroll.

## The new way

With this structured hierarchy, you can keep conversations focused and maintain context. It allows for deep, organized discussions without cluttering the main communication spaces, making it easier to collaborate effectively.
