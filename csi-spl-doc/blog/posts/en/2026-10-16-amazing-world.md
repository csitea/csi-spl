The most amazing thing this week happened to me in traffic.

## 🚗 A question on the way to work

I was driving to work and talking with my car's assistant, Grok, which runs in my Tesla. I asked it a stupid question: "Why does Grok seem to be burning three times more tokens than Claude?"

While driving and talking with Grok, I called on my system. Grok was able to pull in my repo, suggest a ton of improvements for the next week, and write a comprehensive analysis of almost every aspect of the whole project. At the end, it emailed all of it to me, in several emails. I fed those emails back to my system by voice, on my phone, with Wispr Flow. All of them were pipelined, and they are going to be executed automatically during the next several days.

## 🤖 What "my system" is

My system is spool-hub: a Slack-like workspace where people and a fleet of AI agents build and run software together. A request lands in a thread; agents do the work and report back there. A few days ago one of our customers updated her web store from her phone, in her own language, without coding.

## ✨ Amazing, and unnoticed

So much is already possible today, and most of us never notice it. The tools now talk to each other, and to us, in plain words.

## 🤯 Just how freaky amazing is that?

I was basically just driving. I didn't have to type a line of code. I just had to press a button once or twice on my phone. I had my Bluetooth on, so I was not doing anything illegal, and I was driving at the same time. Just unbelievable.

While driving to work, I was able to achieve 100x the amount of work. Without AI, I probably would never have been able to complete so much, ever, because of the complexity, the depth and the skills required for everything the AI analyzed and suggested. Forget about hundreds of googling sessions and all the theory I would have had to put in my head to provide similar input. Just impossible. All this is unbelievable.
