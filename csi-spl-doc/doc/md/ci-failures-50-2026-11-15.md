### T1. Shared Postgres test state (came back: 10-25 S5, `TestPutFXRates`)

21 of 50 runs (D1 14, its D2 cascade 5, F1 2), plus F5.

  `hoursExportGroups` compared member ids as text; once `humans_seq` on the

  channels (`csi-spl-api/src/go/spool-hub-api/internal/hub/marketing_meta_dispatch.go:133`

  counted: unchecked. The mechanism fits: rows persist across groups.
- **F5**: the operator control in `internal/store/cost_read_test.go:228-229`
  wants exactly 2 parts and read a third estate row that another test had
  seeded.

## 5. Urgency formula

**U = reds per week x jobs blocked per red.**

- reds per week = the theme's red runs in the window / 1.32 days x 7.
- jobs blocked per red = the failed jobs of those runs (red + fail-fast
  stops) divided by the runs. A wf 20 red adds 2 (the dev and prd deploys it
  held back), and cascade runs count toward the theme that caused them.

| theme | runs | jobs (+ held deploys) | reds / week | jobs / red | U |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 (D1 + D2 + F1) | 21 | 21 + 10 + 4x2 = 39 | 111.4 | 1.86 | **207** |
| T2 (D5, D6, D7) | 7 | 16 | 37.1 | 2.29 | **85** |
| T4 (D3) | 10 | 10 | 53.0 | 1.00 | **53**, and a known date: 2026-11-27 |
| T3 (D8) | 2 | 6 | 10.6 | 3.00 | **32** |
| T5 (D4) | 5 | 5 | 26.5 | 1.00 | **27** |
| S5 (F2) | 2 | 2 | 10.6 | 1.00 | **11** |
| T6 alloc part (F3) | 2 | 2 | 10.6 | 1.00 | 11, fixed (a286fea4d) |
| F4 | 1 | 1 | 5.3 | 1.00 | 5, fixed (605ac7d6b) |

U is rounded to the nearest whole number (T3 31.8 -> 32, T5 26.5 -> 27,
S5 10.6 -> 11, F4 5.3 -> 5). The job counts (39 for T1, 16 for T2) come from
the editor's job extract and were not rebuilt by a seat.

The rank in section 7 is U first, then order of work: rows 4 and 8 carry
T1's U but come after rows 2 and 3, because row 4 starts with a measurement
that may drop it (rulebook 4.1), and row 8 runs on row 4's `mkdb`. Row 1
removes the F1 shape on its own.

This uses the rate of a fixed defect as the rate of its smell. That is an
assumption, and a 1.32-day window (n=50) is a small sample.
