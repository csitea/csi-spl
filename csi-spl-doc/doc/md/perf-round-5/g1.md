## 2. Practices

Forty practices. A later section says which site they hit, or why they are not worth a change in this round.

1. Read a maintained projection instead of recomputing it on every tick.
2. An incremental add stays O(index probes). A one-line insert does not scan its topic.
3. A maintained counter, with a drift oracle, beats `GROUP BY` plus `count(DISTINCT)` on every GET.
4. Do not recompute a derived column on the steady path.
5. A set-based anti-join, not a per-row `EXISTS`. The sweep's archived probe is already this shape (one index scan, loops=1).
6. Push the top-N under each arm of a `UNION`, then merge.
7. Do not recompute a scalar that a neighbour query already pays for.
8. A list projects the columns the list paints. Wide text stays on the open-one-row read.
9. One round trip per tick. `queryTenantBatch` is already the store's pattern.
10. Seek the unread tail, not the whole history. Landed: flow counts p50 42 to 4 ms (`0fd227cd9`).
11. A covering index so the scan is index-only. Landed: `messages_channel_stats` (`e908f41b`, 147 ms to 96 ms on 180k rows, n=1 in that commit's note).
12. Autovacuum insert scale so an index-only scan stays index-only. Landed as ap-04. Residual heap fetches are below.
13. Fold the follow-up read into the write with `RETURNING`. Landed: round 4 G6.
14. `pgx.Batch` for the statements of one write transaction. Landed: round 4 G3.
15. One CTE instead of lock, then read, then update. Landed: round 4 G5.
16. Memoize a repeated read inside one request. Landed: round 4 G1.
17. Kill an N+1. Landed: round 4 G2.
18. Encode a fan-out once. Landed: round 4 G8.
19. Structs, not `map[string]any`, on a hot frame. Landed: round 4 G12.
20. `sync.Pool` for response buffers. Landed: round 4 G10.
21. No decode, map, re-encode. Landed: round 4 G9.
22. A custom plan only where it was measured faster than the generic plan. Do not prepare everything. The sweep's warm planning was 9.095 ms of a 57.035 ms execution (n=1).
23. A partial index for a poll predicate. `messages_needs_peer` (`0110_messages_claim.sql:54`) is this shape for claims. Not a new action.
24. Keyset pagination. `OFFSET` does not appear in the two list statements this seat proposes to change.
25. Coalesce or debounce only after the burst is measured. ap-06's gate was a median per-member PUT gap. It was not shown, and the statement is not landed.
26. A long read fails at a budget (`search_budget`) instead of scanning without a limit.
27. A `SECURITY DEFINER` door for an index function that is not leakproof. Landed for the search GIN.
28. An operational check has a `statement_timeout`. The sig check has 120 s (`spl-search-sig-check.func.sh:52`), which is still long enough to hold a connection on a 25-connection micro.
29. Migrations are idempotent and forward-only, `lock_timeout` 5 s, as 0143 and 0144 are.
30. A deferred constraint trigger so the head locks are the transaction's last locks (`0144_topic_heads.sql:575`). Keep the deferral. The work inside the trigger has to stay small.
31. Measure, then drop. ap-08 and ap-03 are the precedent: a row with no measured gain is a dropped row, written down.
32. One round trip per request via `queryTenantBatch`, including a read that today is three `queryTenant` calls.
33. Set the RLS scope once per batch. `set_config` is 1 928 568 calls at 0.01 ms (1.7%). The mean is the reason this is not a target.
34. A loose index scan for `DISTINCT` of a low-cardinality key. Flow place keys already walk `min(place_key)` that way (`flow_postgres.go:134`).
35. Do not hold a pooled connection across a multi-second scan on a 25-connection micro.
36. A write-path trigger that costs more than the read it removes is a loss. The insert gate below is this practice.
37. Report the warm `EXPLAIN` and the production mean. They answer different questions.
38. On `db-f1-micro`, bytes touched dominate a clever plan. The sweep is the example.
39. Do not trust the planner's row count under `FORCE RLS`. The sweep key estimated 59 and found 1 971 (n=1).
40. An incremental checkpoint plus a periodic full reconcile. A full recompute every tick is the sweep's shape today.

### 5.1 Incremental unanswered sweep

Practice 1 and practice 40.

Now: 1 473 × 216.81 ms = 319 353.9 ms, 28.3% of DB time. Warm exact statement 57.035 ms, planning 9.095 ms, 15 456 buffer hits, 1 971 topics, width 522, n=1. Key CTE alone 16.442 ms, 13 495 index rows, 1 504 heap fetches, n=1. The cron is about once a minute (1 473 calls in 24 h). E17 (2026-10-04) already narrowed `DISTINCT ON m.*` to the two keys. d-02 (2026-10-07) made the archived probe one hashed `IN` (this plan: 3.158 ms, loops=1, 605 rows). M3 (2026-10-11) added the LATERAL, which ran 57 times in the warm plan.

Change: the minute sweep reads topics whose `topic_heads.changed_at` (or `last_at`, if a changed-at miss is cheaper to prove) is newer than the previous sweep, joins `last_msg_id`, and heap-fetches the body only for the human-last rows the classifier prints. In the warm plan that was 57 of 1 971. Unchanged topics keep the previous sweep's state file, and the classifier re-ages them from that file. A full reconcile of today's statement runs on an hourly cadence, so a backfill in the middle of a topic that does not move the head is still caught. `card_archived` is already on the head.

Expected gain: message inserts in the same window are 847 + 496 = 1 343 a day, under two a minute, and many of those are not a new topic. If a minute touches a handful of topics instead of 1 971 full tuples plus 13 495 index rows, the bytes drop by about two orders and most of the 319 s goes away. The other top statements stop competing for the same cache. The hourly reconcile keeps one 57 ms (warm) or ~217 ms (cold) run, sixty times cheaper than 1 473 runs.

For: the warm plan already shows the LATERAL, the archived `IN` and the agent `EXISTS` are cheap. The cost that remains is pages. Heads exist and are maintained.

Against: a rewrite that only swaps `DISTINCT ON` for `topic_heads` saves ~16 ms warm and little of the 217 ms mean. Row equality is the risk. An expired latest line with an older live line inside the 7-day window, or an archive of a row that is not the head, can disagree with today's statement. The hourly reconcile is what makes that survivable, and it has to be diffed, not assumed.

Proof: the same row set as today's statement, on a fixture and on one day of prd (operator scope, read-only, no row bodies logged). Then the insights mean of this statement, n>=200. Drop the incremental path if that mean stays above ~80 ms: the time was then lock or CPU, not the scan.

### 5.3 Channel counts as a maintained aggregate
