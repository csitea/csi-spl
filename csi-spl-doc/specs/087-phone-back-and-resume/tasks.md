### Phase 2d: The route after an overlay closes
- [x] T012 **the route equals the address bar** (c-582, SPL-5313; found by c-561, msg fb5c5f44): a sheet whose page writes its query through the router while it is open (the issues filter sheet, `router.replace ?status=`) closed with the address bar at `?status=done` but the route at `/issues`.
