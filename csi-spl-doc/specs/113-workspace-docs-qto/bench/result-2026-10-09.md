## 2. adj insert-first: 98 ms, not seat 4's 218-233 ms

It does not land in the 200-ms range. The work is the same (rows below), so the gap is most likely box load, not a smaller case; I did not rerun at load ~26 to isolate it:

- Siblings under the wide parent: 10,000; rows the insert-first's shifting UPDATE touches: 10,000 (L114, `GET DIAGNOSTICS ROW_COUNT` of adj_insert's `UPDATE t_adj SET ord = ord + 1 WHERE parent_id = p AND ord >= 1`, run untimed and rolled back). Append touches 0 of the same 10,000 (L115).
- At 10 siblings the same UPDATE touches 10 rows (L12, L62) and insert-first takes 0.33-1.99 ms (L21-L53, L73-L105). 10,000 rows is ~100x that: the cost is O(siblings of the target parent), as spec 2.1 says.
- Seat 4 measured at load ~26 (spec section 11, seat 4), this run at 10.6-11.8 (L4, last line). The five values are bimodal, 63 ms (L125, L157) and 98-100 ms (L133, L141, L149): scheduling noise on a loaded box, not a different plan.
- The conclusion seat 4 drew holds either way: adj's wide-parent insert-first (98 ms) is the nested set's typical 11k cost (ns insert 224.249 ms median at 11,111 items, same run) or below, and the wide shape costs lt and ns more (160.587 and 207.092 ms).
