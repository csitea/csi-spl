# Questions for the accountants (spec 125)

For the owner to forward as they are (spec [125](spec.md) D9, D10, D11,
section 10 step 1). Please send the answers to the owner only, not to a
chat; account numbers go into the company's own settings, never into a
message.

## Bookkeeping accounts

1. Which bookkeeping account holds the company's bank balance?
2. Which account holds the VAT payable, and is the monthly VAT paid from it
   on a fixed day of the month?
3. Which accounts hold the tax-account liabilities (employer contributions,
   withholding tax, prepayments of income tax), and on which day of the
   month are they paid?

## Cards

4. We have several debit cards and several credit cards. For each credit
   card: do you book its purchases line by line (vendor and cost account per
   purchase), or only the monthly settlement bill as one total? If line by
   line, which account holds the card debt that the settlement pays?
5. For the debit cards: are the card purchases on the bank statement booked
   to their cost accounts automatically (posting rules) or by hand, and how
   many days after the purchase are they usually booked?

## Netvisor

6. Which Netvisor package does the company run today? Does it include API
   access (an integration that only reads data), or is an upgrade needed?
7. Can you, or the company's Netvisor administrator, create a dedicated
   integration user for a read-only integration, so its API identifier does
   not depend on one person's account?
8. Are cost centres (dimensions) used, and which ones should a cost report
   group by?
