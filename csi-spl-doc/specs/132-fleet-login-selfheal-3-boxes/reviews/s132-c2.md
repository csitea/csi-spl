## Gap 1: numbers, proof command

**CHANGE** spec.md:19, the outage list. The three times are not three
outages: 17:45Z is when the login loss began, 18:16-18:22Z is the restore of
10/10 claude seats, 18:31Z is a second restore pass that restarted 8 agents
for nothing (facts relayed by c-001, not measured by this seat). Write them as:
outage 17:45Z to 18:16-18:22Z (31 to 37 min, all claude seats on the box),
plus 8 needless restarts at 18:31Z. Those are the numbers the design must
beat: detection within 1 tick (the cron row `* * * * *`,
`box-crons.manifest:23`), repair within the 10 min cool-down
(`cooldown_min`, `csi-spl-cnf/csi-spl/all.env.yaml:907`), 0 restarts of a seat that
is no longer stuck.

**CHANGE** spec.md:20, "Renewal interval: unmeasured". A first measure
exists: the access token lives ~8 h (479 min, n=1, table above). Put it in,
with n=1, and have the proof record every renewal (the credential's mtime
and expiry, both from `agent-login-cred.sh stat`), so the 48 h run gives
n >= 6 per box.

**CHANGE** spec.md:22-23, the criteria and the command. "0 agent drops" is
not checkable and `do_spl_agent_login_proof` does not exist. Name it as
**to be built** and give its pass line, for example, over 48 h per box:

- the two boxes' sha prefixes DIFFER on every sample (else the run proved
  nothing: they share one credential, as they do today);
- the probe says `ok` on every probe (`LOGIN_PROBE_EVERY` 300 s -> 576
  probes per box in 48 h);
- the expiry advances at least 5 times per box (48 h / ~8 h);
- `stuck_login=0` on every tick of the login check (2880 ticks per box).

Fail = any one of these, with the first failing sample's time.

## Gap 2: refresher down, peer unreachable

**CHANGE** spec.md:25. "Detection relies on the watchdog" does not match the
code, and "peers expire" is still the outcome, not a design. What exists:

- peer unreachable: the refresher's own tick warns ("no credential to
  compare ... there unreachable", `spl-agent-login-check.func.sh:207`) and
  alerts the dispatch lease holder once per state (`_lc_alert`, :331).
- refresher down: nothing runs on it. The peer finds out only when its own
  probe turns `expired`, i.e. after the outage started (:226 then leaves the
  seats alone, rightly).

Missing, and the spec should own it: an **early warning** on every box when
its credential's expiry is closer than a threshold (say 60 min) and not
renewed: `stat` already gives the expiry, so it is one comparison per tick.
That turns "peers expire" into "a person is told 60 min before". **Who
acts:** the dispatch lease holder relays to a person, who runs the vendor's
login on that box; no agent can repair a dead login (owner a7499dc2).

## Gap 6: independent tokens are an assumption

**AGREE** spec.md:14 states the assumption and lets the proof decide.

**CHANGE** spec.md:51, the claude row says copying "Breaks it (independent
tokens per `/login`)" as a fact. That is the unproven assumption, and today's
measure points the other way: both boxes run on one copied credential and
box-1 logged 73/73 ok ticks. Write "unmeasured; the proof decides". The
likely failure of a copy (both boxes' CLIs renewing one refresh token, one
invalidating the other) is also unmeasured: say it is a hypothesis.

**CHANGE** spec.md:24 + :29, a contamination of the proof. While box-1 names
box-2 as a peer, it pushes its credential over box-2's whenever box-2's
expiry is older (`spl-agent-login-check.func.sh:210`). With independent
logins the two expiries leapfrog, so the push would overwrite box-2's own
login every few hours, and the proof would test the copy, not independence.
The `/login` on box-2 and removing `SPOOL_LOGIN_PEERS` on box-1 must be ONE
step, before the 48 h start.
