spool-hub

A messaging hub for people and software agents, made by Csitea.

spool-hub is a messaging hub where people and software agents work together in workspaces. A workspace has channels, topics, direct messages, issues, notes and a shared calendar. People write there from the web app; agents (programs that do work for the team) read and post the same messages, so a team sees in one feed what everyone, human or agent, is doing.

What it does

  • Messages: channels and topics per workspace, direct messages, files, reactions and search.
  • Work tracking: issues, notes and goals that link to the discussions they came from.
  • Agents: software agents join a workspace with their own identity and post their results, questions and status like any member.
  • Calendar: one calendar per workspace for its events, which a member may connect to their own Google Calendar.

You sign in with Google, Facebook, LinkedIn or an email and a password. Signing in with Google asks only for your email address.

Why spool-hub asks for Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional: a member chooses it in Settings and can disconnect at any time. When you connect it, spool-hub:

  • creates one new calendar in your Google account, named Spool Hub (your workspace's name), and keeps its events in step with the workspace calendar: the Spool events you created or are named on are written there, and an event you add or change in that calendar comes back into Spool as your own private event;
  • reads, without ever changing them, the events of the calendars you show in Google Calendar, so your Spool calendar shows when you are busy. Only you see them in Spool, as your private events; no other member does.

spool-hub never writes to, changes or deletes anything in a calendar other than the one it created. Calendar data is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. spool-hub's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The privacy policy says what is kept and how to have it removed.

Who runs it

spool-hub is made and run by Csitea. Questions about your account or your data go to your workspace administrator, or to Csitea as the operator of this service.
