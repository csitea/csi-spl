# Archive

The **Archive** tab (the `🗄` icon in the left rail) collects topics that have
been archived out of the active channel and topic lists, so a finished
conversation can be tidied away without being lost. It holds the archived topics
of your current workspace that you are allowed to read, newest first.

---

## 1. Opening the Archive

Click **Archive** in the left rail, or open `/archive`. The list fills the middle
pane; a **Load more** button pages through older entries.

Each row shows the topic's title, its channel, who started it, its reply count,
and when it was archived.

---

## 2. What you can do with a row

- **Open** — click a row to open that topic and read its full thread, exactly as
  in the live lists.
- **Unarchive** — return the topic to the active channel and topic lists.
- **Delete** — permanently remove the topic. This is available only to the
  topic's author, the workspace owner, and admins, and it asks for confirmation
  first.

> [!NOTE]
> Archiving is a soft delete: an archived topic still exists and can be
> unarchived. Deleting from here is the permanent step.
