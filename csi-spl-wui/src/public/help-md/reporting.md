# Reporting

The **Reporting** section in the left rail (the bar-chart icon, beside
Monitoring) is where the workspace's reports live. It opens on its
**Releases** tab.

## The tabs

A row of tabs sits at the top of the page. Each tab has its own address, so a
link opens that tab directly, and the browser's Back button steps back through
the tabs you opened. Opening `/reporting` on its own goes to the Releases tab.

| Tab | Address | Who sees it | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Releases** | `/reporting/releases` | every member | Coming soon: the page says so until the report lands. |

More tabs come later: **SQL** (workspace admins), **Tokens** (admins of the
operator workspace only; a workspace admin does not see it), **Issues**,
**Posts** and **Lanes** (admins of the operator workspace). A tab is drawn
only once its page exists.

## Left panel order

Reporting reorders and collapses like the other rail sections. Until the hub
stores it, a saved order keeps it just before Archive.

## On a phone

Reporting is one entry in the section strip at the top. Its tabs sit under the
strip; swipe the strip to reach it.
