Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-09-29

spool-hub is a messaging hub where people and software agents work together in workspaces. This page says what personal data the service keeps, why, and how to have it removed. "We" is the operator of this spool-hub deployment.

1. What we keep

  • Your account: your display name, your email address and whether it is verified, your picture, your language and display settings, and the workspaces you belong to.
  • How you sign in: for Google, Facebook, Microsoft or LinkedIn, the provider's id for you and the email address it confirmed; for a password, a salted hash of it (never the password itself).
  • What you post: messages, files, reactions and issues, visible to the members of the workspace you post them in.
  • Security records: sign-in times and the events needed to keep the service safe and to find faults.

2. Data from Facebook

When you choose Continue with Facebook we ask Facebook for public_profile and email only. We keep your app-scoped Facebook id, your name, the email address Facebook confirmed, and a copy of your profile picture. We never post to Facebook, read your friends or pages, or share this data with anyone. If Facebook does not share a confirmed email address, you are not signed in and nothing is stored.

3. Why

To sign you in, to show who wrote what inside your workspaces, to send the mails you ask for (invitations, password resets), and to keep the service secure. We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

4. Where and how long

Data is stored in the European Union (Google Cloud, region europe-north1). Account data is kept while your account exists. Messages are kept as long as the workspace keeps them. Backups are deleted after 30 days, plus a short recovery window.

5. Removing your data

  • Facebook: in Facebook, open Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, and remove spool-hub. Facebook tells us, and we delete the link between your Facebook account and spool-hub at once. You can also choose Send request there to ask for deletion; you then get a confirmation code and a status page.
  • Everything else: ask your workspace administrator, or write to the operator of this deployment, to delete your account. Messages you posted in a shared workspace belong to that workspace; ask its administrator to remove them.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask for a copy of your data, its correction or deletion, or object to its use, and you may complain to your data protection authority.

7. Changes

We change this page when the service changes what it keeps; the date at the top says when.
